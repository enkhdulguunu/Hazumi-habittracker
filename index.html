ABOUT US

Hazumi is a comprehensive productivity and self-management platform designed to help individuals cultivate sustainable habits, organize daily responsibilities, and optimize their use of time. Built upon principles of behavioral psychology and productivity science, Hazumi provides an integrated environment where users can systematically track progress, maintain accountability, and achieve both personal and professional goals.

Our Core Features

The Habit Tracker enables users to monitor and evaluate recurring behaviors that contribute to long-term success. Whether the objective is improving health, increasing knowledge, or developing positive routines, users can record daily progress and visualize consistency over time.

The To-Do List provides a structured framework for managing daily tasks and responsibilities. Users can prioritize activities, track completion status, and maintain clarity regarding short-term objectives.

The Time Block feature allows users to allocate specific periods of time to planned activities. By assigning dedicated time slots to tasks and goals, users can improve concentration, reduce distractions, and maximize productivity.

Our vision

At Hazumi, we believe that extraordinary achievements are the result of small, consistent actions performed every day. Through the integration of habit formation, task organization, and strategic time management, we aim to help users build momentum, maintain focus, and achieve sustainable personal development.
