Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information HAZUMI collects, how it is used, and where it is stored while you use the website.

This is a starter privacy policy for the HAZUMI project. If HAZUMI is used as a public commercial service, review this policy with a qualified legal professional before launch.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as full name, email address, and password hash.
  • Optional profile information such as phone number, date of birth, and gender.
  • Productivity content such as todos, habits, calendar entries, notes, and time blocks.
  • Session information needed to keep you logged in.

2. How We Use Information

HAZUMI uses your information to create and protect your account, show your dashboard, save your productivity data, and keep each user's content separate from other users.

3. Storage

  • Account data is stored in the HAZUMI MySQL database.
  • Todos on the dedicated To-do List page are stored in MySQL by user account.
  • Dashboard widgets, todos, habits, habit logs, calendar entries, notes, and time blocks are stored in MySQL by user account.
  • Session cookies are used so the website can remember that you are logged in.

4. Passwords

HAZUMI stores passwords as password hashes, not plain text passwords. You should still use a strong password and avoid sharing it with anyone.

5. Sharing

HAZUMI does not sell your personal information. Information is used for the website's features and is not intentionally shared with other users.

6. Your Choices

You can log out at any time. For account data questions or deletion requests, contact the HAZUMI project owner or website administrator.

7. Security

HAZUMI uses sessions and database access rules to help protect user data. No website can guarantee perfect security, so keep your login information private and use trusted devices when possible.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the HAZUMI project owner or website administrator.
